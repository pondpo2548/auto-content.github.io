Privacy Policy – Thai Shorts Uploader

Effective date: 29 October 2026

Thai Shorts Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels PsychologyLabTH and GeoTH to upload videos that the owner created to the owner's own YouTube channels. Each channel is authorized separately, and each upload goes only to the channel that was authorized for it.

Google user data we access

The app requests only the YouTube Data API scope youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the authorized channels. It does not read, collect, or store any other Google account data such as email, contacts, viewing history, or channel analytics.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are used solely to upload videos. No data is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties, and none is used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The tokens stay on the owner's computer until they are deleted or access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
